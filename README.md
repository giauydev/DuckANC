# DuckANC

**Automatically reduce audio from other tabs and stay focused on studying and working — with an AirPods-style ANC effect.**

DuckANC is a Chrome extension that automatically reduces the volume of other browser tabs when your active tab starts playing audio, helping you stay focused without completely muting your other tabs.
**P/S: Perfect for those who want to listen to music while still being able to hear lectures without getting distracted.**

## ✨ Features

* 🎧 Automatically reduces audio from other tabs
* 🔊 Keeps your active tab at normal volume
* 🌙 AirPods-style ANC-inspired audio experience
* ⚡ Smooth audio transitions
* 🔔 Optional AirPods-style sound effects
* 💾 Saves your settings locally
* 🖥️ Designed for Google Chrome

## 📥 Installation

### 1. Download DuckANC

Go to the **Releases** section of this repository and download the latest DuckANC release.

Extract the downloaded `.zip` file to a folder on your computer.

> **Important:** Do not delete the extracted folder after installing the extension. Chrome loads the extension directly from this folder.

### 2. Open Chrome Extensions

Open Google Chrome and go to:

`chrome://extensions/`

Or:

**Menu → Extensions → Manage Extensions**

### 3. Enable Developer Mode

Turn on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.

### 4. Load DuckANC

Click **Load unpacked**.

Select the **DuckANC folder** that you extracted from the Release ZIP.

The extension should now appear in your Chrome extensions list.

### 5. Pin DuckANC

Click the puzzle-piece **Extensions** icon in Chrome and pin **DuckANC** for quick access.

## ⚙️ Usage

Once installed, DuckANC works automatically in the background.

When another tab starts playing audio while you are listening to your active tab, DuckANC automatically reduces the volume of the other tab.

You can open the DuckANC popup to enable or disable the extension and configure available options.

## 🗑️ Uninstall

Go to:

`chrome://extensions/`

Find **DuckANC**, click **Remove**, and confirm.
